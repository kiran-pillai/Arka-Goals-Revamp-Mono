# Guidelines & Procedures

This page outlines Cheetah Squad's attendance requirements, accountability standards, and important member resources.

## 1. Attendance Policy

- **Excused Absences:** Members are permitted up to two excused absences per calendar quarter.
- **Exceeding the Quarterly Allowance:** The third absence and each subsequent absence within a calendar quarter will result in a strike, regardless of whether the absence is excused.
- **Unexcused Absences:** Any unexcused absence will result in an immediate strike.
- **Two Strikes:** Accumulating two strikes within a calendar year will trigger a group discussion with the member to determine whether continued participation is a good fit.
- **Three Strikes:** Accumulating three strikes within a calendar year constitutes grounds for dismissal from the group.
- **Annual Reset:** All accumulated strikes expire on January 1 of each year.
- **Reporting Absences:** Members must notify the Captain and complete the Missed Meeting Form in advance, as soon as they become aware of an anticipated absence.

For further details, refer to the [Official Attendance Policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WhTFFemn8buNKcd-PWSTYWDojR6NDd_M-eji3Ik0TLA/edit?tab=t.0).

## 2. Meeting Punctuality

- Members are expected to arrive on time for all meetings.
- Members arriving late are required to perform push-ups to failure upon arrival.

## 3. Goal Submission Deadlines

- **Deadline:** Weekly goals must be submitted by Monday at 12:00 PM Central Time.
- **Late Submissions:** Members who miss the deadline are required to perform push-ups to failure at the meeting.
- **Multiple Infractions:** Members who both submit their goals late and arrive late to the meeting must perform two separate sets of push-ups to failure:
  - One upon arrival.
  - One during the meeting break.
