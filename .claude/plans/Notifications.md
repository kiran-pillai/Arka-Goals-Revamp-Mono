### Purpose
Whenever a comment is left under a users check in we want the user to receive notification. Ex: Anthony Wilson left comment on your check in from October 1st.

### UI
There should be a bubble on the top right hand side of the user avatar with a number of how many unread notifications they have. When clicking on the avatar a dropdown menu with all notifications will be shown. When users click on a specific notification they will be autoscrolled to the checkin. There will also be options to mark notifications as read on the right hand side.
