# Guidelines & Procedures

## Summary

The document requests a new final tab named “Guidelines & Procedures” containing Cheetah Squad's attendance requirements, accountability standards, and member resources. No author or document date is stated. [Source: opening instruction and introduction](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md)

## Key points

- Members may have up to **two excused absences per calendar quarter**. The third absence and every subsequent absence in that quarter results in a strike, regardless of whether it is excused. Any unexcused absence results in an immediate strike. [Source: §1, Excused Absences; Exceeding the Quarterly Allowance; Unexcused Absences](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#1-attendance-policy)
- **Two strikes within a calendar year** trigger a group discussion with the member about whether continued participation is a good fit. **Three strikes within a calendar year** constitute grounds for dismissal. All accumulated strikes expire on **January 1 each year**. [Source: §1, Two Strikes; Three Strikes; Annual Reset](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#1-attendance-policy)

## Asks and obligations

- Create a new tab as the **last tab**, named exactly **“Guidelines & Procedures”**, with the source's content. [Source: opening instruction](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md)
- For an anticipated absence, members must **notify the Captain and complete the Missed Meeting Form in advance**, as soon as they become aware of it. [Source: §1, Reporting Absences](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#1-attendance-policy)
- Members are expected to arrive on time for all meetings. A member arriving late must perform **push-ups to failure upon arrival**. [Source: §2](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#2-meeting-punctuality)
- Weekly goals must be submitted by **Monday at 12:00 PM Central Time**. Missing the deadline requires **push-ups to failure at the meeting**. [Source: §3, Deadline; Late Submissions](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#3-goal-submission-deadlines)
- A member who both submits goals late and arrives late must perform **two separate sets of push-ups to failure**: one upon arrival and one during the meeting break. [Source: §3, Multiple Infractions](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#3-goal-submission-deadlines)
- Include the following member resources and their stated purposes. [Source: §4](../../.agents/artifacts/guidelines-procedures.md#4-member-resources)
  - [Arka Member Portal](https://my.arkabrotherhood.com): access Arka membership resources and information.
  - [Missed Meeting Form](https://forms.gle/2WMy6A3VRR5jb5X49): report anticipated absences in advance.
  - [Official Attendance Policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WhTFFemn8buNKcd-PWSTYWDojR6NDd_M-eji3Ik0TLA/edit?tab=t.0): review official attendance requirements and procedures; §1 also directs readers here for further details.
  - [Attendance Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-qiBksbw1Xp53c3MSe_uZUgL_XozPcQPz2bOiahlPBA/edit?gid=77398055#gid=77398055): view attendance records and track absences.
